- yusAgent: over ACP, every turn now opens with `turnStart` in the bridge `_meta` (`origin` `prompt` or `wake`, with the wake's kind), and a turn the agent starts on its own (a `[bash-done]` notice, a heartbeat, a goal, a quota resume, another client's prompt) gets its own turn id and ends with a response boundary and the completion envelope. A prompt sent during such a turn runs once it ends. Stop during such a turn stops only that turn and keeps the queued notices. The VS Code extension reads these signals instead of guessing the start of a turn. Nothing changes in what the model sees.
