- yusAgent: the TUI answers the permission requests of a manual session: a panel shows the whole tool call and takes `y` (allow once), `n` or `Esc` (deny once) after a short pause in typing, `Ctrl+C` withdraws the request at once; a denied call does not run and the turn stops. `/permissions` switches the session between `manual` and `auto` (journaled), and the tray shows `auto · no sandbox` while auto is on. Sessions created by the TUI are no longer refused in manual. A client that answers and leaves by a direct worker link no longer leaves its request waiting.
